A closer look

Dashboard

Your money situation, in one glance

Open the app and see exactly what's outstanding, what's overdue, and what needs attention today. Invoices are grouped by urgency so the oldest debt is always on top.

  • Total unpaid and overdue at the top
  • Overdue, Due Soon, and Awaiting Payment groups
  • Client leaderboard by outstanding balance
Reminders

Nudged before you'd even think to look

Every unpaid invoice gets four notifications automatically. Each one names the client and amount so you know exactly what it's about without opening the app.

  • Day before due, day of, 3 days late, 1 week late
  • Delivered at your chosen hour, every time
  • Reminders disappear the moment you mark it paid
Follow-ups

The message is already written

Tap once and a polite, professional follow-up appears with the invoice number, amount, and due date filled in. Open it in Mail or copy it to paste wherever you talk to your clients.

  • Pre-filled with invoice and client details
  • Opens directly in Mail or copy to clipboard
  • Professional tone, no awkward drafting
Clients

A smart client list that does the math

Store contact details, preferred payment terms, and communication preferences for each client. New invoices inherit their terms so the due date is always right.

  • Email, phone, and contact preferences stored
  • Default payment terms auto-fill new invoices
  • Full history and outstanding balance per client
Archive

A record of everything that came in

Paid invoices move to a searchable archive with a running total. See what a client paid over a year, or what a quarter actually brought in — without a spreadsheet.

  • All paid invoices with timestamps
  • Running totals by client and period
  • Instant payment history at your fingertips
Overdue Tracker

See everything that's late, sorted by age

The overdue list is sorted by due date so the oldest outstanding invoice is always first. You always know who's been sitting on your money the longest.

  • Sorted by due date, oldest first
  • Overdue amount shown at a glance
  • One tap to send a follow-up
